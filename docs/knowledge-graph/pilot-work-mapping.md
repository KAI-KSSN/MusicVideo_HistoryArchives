## Evidence policy

- Every relationship has at least one source link in a private source-association table.
- Directly observable Visual Language claims use the official video as primary evidence.
- Technology and construction claims use production, trade, equipment-maker, or practitioner sources.
- Public clients can read the verified relationship views but cannot read verification notes or source-link tables.

## Deliberately unresolved

- Thriller is not linked to Steadicam in this sprint because no sufficiently direct source was attached to the claim.
- Virtual Insanity is not linked to Motion Control. The visual construction does not establish a programmable repeatable camera system.
- Single Ladies is mapped to Continuous Shot Illusion rather than literal One Take.
- Weapon of Choice and 新宝島 have no Technology relationship until suitable production documentation is attached.
- Lemon and One Last Kiss remain entirely unlinked. This is intentional, not missing data.
- Historical influence relationships are outside this sprint unless the direction of influence is explicit and sourced.

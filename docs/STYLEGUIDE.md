# Data and Writing Style Guide

## Titles

Use the official title.

Do not add unnecessary suffixes such as:

- Official Video
- Official Music Video
- Full Version

unless they are part of the official title.

## Artist Names

Use the officially credited artist name.

Examples:

- Beyoncé
- a-ha
- 米津玄師
- サカナクション

## People and Company Names

Use the official or most commonly credited form.

Store alternate spellings separately when needed.

## Years

Use the first official release year of the music video.

## Awards

Store separately:

- Award organization
- Category
- Result
- Award year

## Production Credits

Do not assume that a company is the main production company simply because it appears in a case study.

Distinguish where possible:

- Production Company
- Service Production
- Agency
- VFX Production
- Post Production
- Animation Studio

## Editorial Tone

Use neutral, concise language.

Prefer:

> The video combines live action with rotoscope animation.

Avoid:

> This incredible masterpiece completely changed everything.

# Music Video History Library — Vision

Music Video History Library is a curated archive for learning the history of music videos and discovering references for future work.

## Purpose

This project has two core purposes:

1. To function as a history textbook for important music videos.
2. To function as a reference library for active filmmakers and visual creators.

The goal is not to collect every music video ever made.

The goal is to preserve and organize works that are historically, technically, culturally, or creatively significant.

## Long-term Vision

The system should remain extensible as new works, technologies, and visual languages continue to emerge.

Future expansion may include:

- Commercial films
- Feature films
- Short films
- Title sequences
- Fashion films
- Live visuals
- Experimental moving-image works

## Core Principles

- Historical context matters.
- Credits and sources must be verifiable.
- New techniques should be addable without redesigning the database.
- The archive should support both beginners and professionals.
- The service should not impose a fixed curriculum.
- Companies, schools, and individuals may use the archive in their own way.

# Editorial Policy

## Editorial Goal

MVHL documents music videos that should not be overlooked when studying moving-image history.

It is not a review site and does not rank works by popularity or personal taste.

## Inclusion Criteria

A work may be included when it satisfies one or more of the following:

- Historical significance
- Technical innovation
- Cultural impact
- Major award recognition
- Influence on later works
- Representative work of an important director
- Representative work of an important artist
- Frequently referenced within the visual-production industry
- Exceptional production, cinematography, editing, animation, or VFX craft

## Editorial Writing

Editorial text should clearly distinguish between:

- Confirmed fact
- Editorial interpretation
- Unverified information

Avoid promotional language and unsupported claims.

## Verification

Director, production, VFX, award, and release information should be verified using reliable sources whenever possible.

Preferred sources include:

- Official artist or label pages
- Official video descriptions
- Director portfolios
- Production-company pages
- VFX-company pages
- Award archives
- Reputable publications

## Publication Status

Suggested workflow:

- candidate
- researching
- verified
- published
- archived
- rejected

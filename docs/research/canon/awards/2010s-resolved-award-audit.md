# 2010s resolved-work award audit

Completed: 2026-07-13

## Scope

This audit covers the 15 resolved Canon works in the `2010s-*` evidence files whose award audits were in progress. `This Is America` and `Lemon` retain their completed pilot audits. `Arcade Fire — Reflektor` remains blocked by identity resolution and is deliberately not marked complete.

All 23 required MVHL award programs were checked. Registration requires an exact work identity, program, year, category, outcome, and official source. Empty results are intentional where no such record was found.

## Existing verified results retained

Existing exact official records were retained for `Never Catch Me`, `Chandelier`, `Happy`, `Blackstar`, `Magic of Love`, `Obsession`, `ファッションモンスター`, `Needing / Getting`, `Wide Open`, `忘却`, and `Let It Happen`.

## Deliberate exclusions

- `Blackstar`, `Can't Feel My Face`, and other same-title GRAMMY records for audio recordings, songs, performances, or albums were excluded from the music-video work ledger.
- The Webby record for `Just a Reflektor` was not attached to the unresolved `Arcade Fire — Reflektor` spreadsheet identity.
- Person-level award biographies were not treated as work awards.
- Similar-title results were rejected.

## No additional result registered

No additional exact work-level result was safe to register for `Black Lake`, `Can't Feel My Face`, `Lift`, or `Daydreaming`. Their empty award arrays are valid completed outcomes.

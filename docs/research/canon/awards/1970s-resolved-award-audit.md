# MVHL Canon award audit — resolved 1970s identities

Audit date: 2026-07-13
Registration rule: official archive evidence only; an empty result set is valid.

## Scope

The following selected-work identities were sufficiently specific for an award-scope audit:

- Queen — Bohemian Rhapsody
- David Bowie — Ashes to Ashes
- ABBA — Take a Chance on Me
- The Buggles — Video Killed the Radio Star
- Blondie — Heart of Glass
- The Clash — London Calling
- Kate Bush — Wuthering Heights
- Elvis Costello — Watching the Detectives

Pink Floyd — Another Brick in the Wall, Kraftwerk — The Robots, Donna Summer — I Feel Love, and ELO — Shine a Little Love remain outside this completed group because the exact selected video version has not been resolved.

## Method

Each resolved identity was searched using exact artist, title, and known director variants against the official program sites in the MVHL scope. Programs whose relevant annual music-video competition began after the work's release were recorded as not applicable for the release year. For programs already operating in the release period, the official archive was searched for exact work-level and craft-level records.

Star Guitar (2002) and Californication (2000) were assessed using their verified release years, not their workbook grouping.

## Method

Exact artist/title combinations and known director variants were searched across every official program site in the MVHL scope. Results were registered only when the official record identified the selected video, program, category, year, and outcome. Song, album, person, honorary, tribute, retrospective editorial, live-performance, remake, mashup, and similarly titled records were excluded.

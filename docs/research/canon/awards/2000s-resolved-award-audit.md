# 2000s resolved-work award audit

Completed: 2026-07-13

## Scope

This audit covers the 15 resolved Canon works in the `2000s-*` evidence files whose award audits were not already complete. `Weapon of Choice` and `Single Ladies` retain their earlier completed pilot audits.

Every work was checked against the 23 required MVHL award programs. Searches prioritized awarding-body archives and official corporate archives. Search snippets, Wikipedia, fan databases, and unsourced portfolio claims were not used as registration evidence. A missing result remains an empty result.

## Deliberate exclusions and false-positive controls

- Kanye West — Stronger: the official GRAMMY result is Best Rap Solo Performance, not a music-video award.
- Kylie Minogue — Come Into My World: the official GRAMMY result is Best Dance Recording, not a music-video award.
- Radiohead — House of Cards: the simultaneous GRAMMY rock-performance nomination is not a work-level music-video result.
- OK Go — Here It Goes Again: an official 2009 MTV promotion references a fan performance of the song; it is not a retrospective award for the original video.
- Similar-title results for unrelated works were rejected.

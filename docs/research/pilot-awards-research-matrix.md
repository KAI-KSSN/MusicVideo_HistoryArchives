# Pilot Awards Research Matrix

Status values:

- `pending` — not yet searched to the required depth.
- `searched_no_verified_result` — searched using the required name variants and no registrable official evidence was found.
- `verified_results` — one or more official-source-backed relationships were found.
- `unresolved` — a claim exists but the available evidence is insufficient or conflicting.
- `not_applicable` — the program/category did not exist or could not apply in the relevant period; the reason must be recorded.

An empty `work_award_results` relationship is always valid. Negative findings
belong in this report only and must never be inserted as award rows.

## Recording requirements per completed cell

For each work and research group, record:

1. exact queries and title/name variants used;
2. official archive pages checked;
3. result found, no verified result found, unresolved, or not applicable;
4. official category label and ceremony year;
5. exact outcome type;
6. credited recipient where the result is craft/person/company-specific;
7. source URL and access date;
8. why any list-external program is relevant;
9. whether the record is an award, nomination, festival selection, jury selection, editorial selection, or platform recognition.
